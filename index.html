How it works

Here's how it works

I.

Fill in the form

It takes about ten minutes and there's no call. I ask about your budget, guest numbers, when you're thinking, which countries you like and the sort of place you picture, whether that's a villa, a castle, something by the beach or a vineyard.

I've got brochures, quotes and emails from hundreds of venues, and I add to them every time I travel. I pick the ones that fit your numbers and your style, and I always leave room in the budget for everything that comes after the venue.

Questions

Things people ask me

Are you a wedding planner?

No. I find and shortlist venues. Once you've got your shortlist, you get in touch with the venues yourself and the planning is yours (or your planner's).

Do you contact the venues for me?

No. Each entry tells you who to email and what to ask, so your first message is a good one. Booking directly also means it's just you and the venue, no middle person.

Why is there no call?

Because the form tells me everything I need, and it keeps the price down. You do it once, whenever suits you, and I work from that. If you have questions once your shortlist arrives, just reply to the email.

Where do the prices come from?

From the venues. Brochures, quotes and emails from over 400 enquiries, and I keep updating them as I travel. Prices do move with the season and the year, so each entry tells you what to double-check when you email.

Which countries do you cover?

Anywhere in Europe. Italy, Greece, Spain and France come up the most, but I've looked at Portugal, Albania and plenty of others too. Just put the place on the form.

Is the fee the cost of the venue?

No, the fee is for the research. The actual hire fees and catering prices for each venue are in your shortlist, so you know what you'd be paying before you email anyone.
